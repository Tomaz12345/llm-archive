Continued sessions

A resumed conversation does not always keep writing into the transcript it started in: Claude Code's --resume opens a new one and replays the old one into it. That makes two session rows out of one conversation, and the replayed prefix would be counted twice in every figure above. The copies are detected by shared message ids and excluded — re-run with llma lineage.
