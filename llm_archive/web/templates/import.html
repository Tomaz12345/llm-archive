Add an export

Drop a file here, or pick one. Exports are identified by their contents, not their names — Grok's is a bare uuid, Mistral's is a timestamp — so you do not have to know which is which. Re-adding one you already hold does nothing.

Or from a terminal: llma add ~/Downloads — same identification, and it leaves your originals where they are.
